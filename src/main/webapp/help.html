User guide

Standard Clinic Workflow

The order a patient visit moves through the system

  1. Sign in Use your own staff account. The menu shows only the areas your role is allowed to open.
  2. Find or register the patient Search the directory first, so the same person is not entered twice.
  3. Book the appointment Choose the patient, dentist, treatment, date and time. Double bookings are blocked automatically.
  4. Check the patient in On arrival, move the appointment to Checked in so the dentist can start the session.
  5. Record the treatment The dentist starts the session and saves the clinical record, which completes the appointment.
  6. Create the bill and take payment Raise the bill for the completed appointment, record the payment and print the receipt.
  7. Sign out Always sign out when you finish, especially on a shared computer.

Using Each Module

What each area does and who can open it

Patients

Administrator, Reception

Register new patients, search the directory and open a full patient record.

  • Search before registering anyone new.
  • Record an accurate contact number.
  • Deactivate rather than delete old records.

Appointments

Administrator, Reception

Book and reschedule visits, then move them through confirmed, checked in, completed or cancelled.

  • Monday to Saturday, 08:00 to 18:00.
  • Times run in 15-minute steps.
  • A cancellation needs a reason.

Treatments

Administrator, Dentist

Start sessions for checked-in patients and write the clinical record for the visit.

  • The patient must be checked in first.
  • Diagnosis and treatment given are required.
  • One record only, per appointment.

Billing

Administrator, Cashier

Raise bills for completed appointments, record payments and print bills and receipts.

  • Only completed, unbilled visits are listed.
  • A discount needs a written reason.
  • Record only money actually received.

Reports

Administrator only

Summarise appointments, patients, treatments and revenue for a date range, and print the result.

  • Choose both the from and to dates.
  • Check the totals before printing.
  • Keep printed reports secure.

Email notifications

Automatic — no page to open

The system emails the patient by itself when a visit or payment is recorded. There is no inbox for staff to check.

  • Sent when an appointment is booked, rescheduled or cancelled.
  • Sent when a treatment is completed, a bill is created or a payment is received.
  • An email is not proof of payment — check the bill in Billing.

Common Problems

Select a question to see what to do

I cannot sign in

Check the username and password, and make sure Caps Lock is off. Accounts are disabled rather than deleted when staff leave, so ask the administrator if the details look correct.

A page sends me back to the sign-in screen

Your session has ended, either because you signed out or because it expired. Sign in again and reopen the page.

A menu item I expected is missing

The menu only lists the areas your role can open. Check the access table above; if the area should be available to you, ask the administrator to review your role.

An appointment will not save

The time must be Monday to Saturday, between 08:00 and 18:00, in 15-minute steps, and neither the dentist nor the patient may already be booked then. Read the message shown under the form.

I cannot start or complete a treatment session

The patient must be checked in before a session can start, and a session must be in treatment before it can be completed. Reception can check the patient in from Appointments.

An appointment is missing from Create Bill

Only completed appointments that have not been billed yet appear there. Confirm the treatment record was saved, and search Billing in case the bill already exists.

The report is empty

Check the date range. A report shows zero results when there was no clinic activity in that period.

Recent changes are not showing

Refresh the page. If it still looks out of date, press Ctrl + F5 to reload the latest files from the server.

Safe System Use

Protecting patient and payment information

  • Never share your username or password, and never work under someone else's account.
  • Check patient details on screen before saving any change.
  • Do not leave the system open on an unattended computer.
  • Do not discuss or pass on patient, clinical or billing information to anyone outside the clinic.
  • Keep printed bills, receipts and reports secure, and dispose of them properly.
  • Report wrong information or anything suspicious to the clinic administrator.
