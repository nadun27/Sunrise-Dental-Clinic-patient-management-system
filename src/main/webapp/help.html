Help and User Guide

Instructions for using the patient management system safely and correctly.

Dashboard

Quick start

Standard Clinic Workflow

  1. Sign in using your authorized staff account.
  2. Find or register the patient before creating an appointment.
  3. Create the appointment with the dentist, treatment, date and time.
  4. Update the appointment when it is completed or cancelled.
  5. Generate the bill and record the patient’s payment.
  6. Review management reports if your account has administrator access.
  7. Log out when you finish using the system.

System modules

How to Use Each Module

01

Patients

Use the patient module to register new patients, search existing records and review patient details.

  • Check for an existing patient first.
  • Enter accurate contact information.
  • Do not create duplicate patient records.
02

Appointments

Create and manage appointments using the selected patient, dentist, treatment, date and time.

  • Confirm the patient and dentist.
  • Check the selected appointment time.
  • Update the status after the appointment.
03

Treatments

Authorized administrators and dentists can manage the clinic’s treatment information and prices.

  • Use clear treatment names.
  • Check the price before saving.
  • Do not remove an active treatment carelessly.
04

Billing

Generate bills from appointment and treatment information, then record received payments.

  • Verify the patient and treatment.
  • Check the total before generating the bill.
  • Record only payments actually received.
05

Notifications

System notifications provide information about important appointment, treatment and billing events.

  • Read new notifications carefully.
  • Confirm important changes with clinic records.
  • Do not treat a notification as a payment receipt.
06

Reports

Administrators can generate reports for a selected date range and print them when required.

  • Select both the from and to dates.
  • Review totals before printing.
  • Store printed reports securely.

Problem solving

Common Problems

I cannot sign in

Check the username and password carefully. Confirm that Caps Lock is off. If the problem continues, contact the clinic administrator.

A page returns to the login screen

Your session may have ended, or you may have logged out. Sign in again and reopen the required page.

I cannot see Treatments or Reports

These functions depend on the signed-in staff role. Treatment management is limited to authorized roles, while management reports require administrator access.

An appointment cannot be created

Check all required fields and verify the selected date, time, dentist, patient and treatment. Read the validation message displayed by the system.

The report contains no information

Check the selected date range. A report may contain zero results when no clinic activity exists within that period.

Recent changes are not visible

Refresh the page. If necessary, use Ctrl + F5 to reload the latest page files from the server.

Data protection

Safe System Use

  • Never share your username or password with another person.
  • Check patient information before saving any change.
  • Do not leave the system open on an unattended computer.
  • Do not disclose patient or billing information to unauthorized people.
  • Log out after completing your work.
  • Report incorrect information or suspicious activity to the clinic administrator.
